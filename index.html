Three Scenarios, Three Moderation Outcomes

Not all negative posts should be removed. CampusConnect protects lawful criticism (Scenario 14), flags questionable accusations for human review (Scenario 5), and removes knowingly false reposts with explanation and appeal (Scenario 11).

Allow Post

Scenario 14 • Tech Blog Criticism

What happened?
A student tech blog criticized the newly deployed campus app's latency and security flaws, attaching benchmarks and patch recommendations.
Lawful, defamatory or protected?
Protected expression: negative opinion and evidence-based product review, not a defamatory imputation against a person.
Concepts applied
Fair comment on public interest, no personal identifiability or malice, factual documentation attached.
CampusConnect action
Allow Post without restriction; reject takedown demands made solely over negative technical feedback.
Flag for Review

Scenario 5 • Unverified Rumor

What happened?
A student republished an anonymous rumor naming Registrar employee Mr. Hector Dizon for manipulating final grades without proof.
Lawful, defamatory or protected?
Questionable accusation: names an identifiable employee and alleges dishonesty without verified facts, requiring human review.
Concepts applied
Defamatory imputation, identifiability, republication, reckless disregard vs. good-faith verification.
CampusConnect action
Display content warning and hold for human moderator verification before deciding whether to allow or restrict.
Remove / Restrict

Scenario 11 • False Repost Claim

What happened?
While reposting, a user added a newly authored, knowingly false accusation claiming Mr. Ramon Santos embezzled ₱50,000.
Lawful, defamatory or protected?
Defamatory: adding a new false crime accusation when sharing creates independent liability under Chapter VI.
Concepts applied
Authoring new defamatory text on repost, actual malice (knowledge of falsity), identifiability, due process.
CampusConnect action
Remove / Restrict after human review, issue a written explanation citing policy, and provide a formal appeal option.
